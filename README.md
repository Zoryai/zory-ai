# ZORY AI | Visual Commerce & PropTech

B2B2C AI enablement layer for furniture commerce and real estate, connecting AI visualization, room planning, product discovery, and AI agents to help businesses improve engagement, conversion, and sales.>

## Overview
ZORY AI is a B2B2C AI-powered enablement layer for furniture commerce and real estate marketing. We connect furniture and design to give furniture and decor retailers, real estate developers, and interior designers a visual commerce layer that transforms how spaces are marketed and personalized.
ZORY enables businesses present their products and spaces within realistic customer environments. Retailers showcase their own products inside customers' actual rooms; developers design complete residential units using real products with budget estimation, quantity calculations, and furnishing directly from a floor plan. The result: stronger customer engagement, higher purchase confidence, better conversion, and faster sales.
Built for businesses and simplified for everyone, ZORY unifies AI design, visual commerce, product discovery, room planning, and AI-powered sales into one connected platform.
From prompts or sketches to design, room redesign, furniture visualization, floor planning, visual search, and AI sales agents, ZORY offers a complete suite of AI tools that simplify how spaces are designed, furnished, visualized, and sold.
